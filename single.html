The salvation Message

The Inner Court was not a place for everyone. Only those who had been cleansed, consecrated and set apart could draw near. Today that veil has been torn not by human hands but by the sacrifice of Jesus Christ on the cross. Salvation is the door into the Inner Court of God's presence. It is not earned by works, not purchased by religion, not inherited by birth. It is received by grace through faith.

The moment you confess with your mouth and believe in your heart that Jesus Christ is Lord, the way into the Holy of Holies swings open and you are welcomed not as a stranger but as a son, not as a servant but as an heir. The smoke of the incense rises. The menorah burns. The presence of God fills the room. This is what salvation opens not just a better life, but access to the living God Himself.
